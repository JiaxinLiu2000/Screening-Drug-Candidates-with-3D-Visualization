## 4. What this verification does NOT cover

Stating the limits is part of the verification; a check that is not run is not a check that passed.

- **External validity.** Every number is measured on these datasets with these splits. Nothing here says the models generalise to new chemistry beyond what the scaffold split already probes.
- **Bootstrap scope.** The endpoint confidence intervals resample test molecules only. They do not include retraining variance or the variance of choosing a different split protocol.
- **Neural training budget.** Configurations D, E and F hit their epoch cap in every run, so their numbers are a lower bound and any comparison against the uncapped GIN configurations is budget-mismatched.
- **Loss-weight grid.** 2 seeds per cell for most weights; for the pretrained encoder the seed spread exceeds the weight spread, so the grid bounds the effect rather than locating an optimum.
- **Docking.** No docking has been run. The benchmark is assembled and documented only, and its active structures are systematically lower resolution than its inactive ones.
- **Attribution ground truth.** There is none. The structural-alert comparison is a proxy, validated against a random same-size-group null.
- **Stereochemistry.** Dropped in v1 standardization, so stereo-dependent toxicity cannot be expressed by any model here.
