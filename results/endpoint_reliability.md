## Bootstrap construction

For each split and seed, `rng = np.random.default_rng(10000 + seed)` draws 1000 index vectors with replacement over that split's test molecules. Resample *b* is shared by all 12 endpoints; each endpoint is scored on the labelled molecules in draw *b* with the fit's fixed predictions (no refitting). The seed-pooled statistic for *b* is the mean over the 5 seeds of the per-seed value for *b*; the macro statistic averages the 12 endpoints within each seed first. The difference for *b* is random macro_b minus scaffold macro_b, paired by seed and resample index. CIs are the 2.5th/97.5th percentiles over the 1000 *b*. These CIs capture test-set sampling variability only, conditional on the fitted models and the 5 splits.
