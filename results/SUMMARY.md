**4. PDB ID to use: 1M17** (over 4HJO)

   Decision criteria, in order: no unobserved residue inside the 5 A pocket (hard gate, both pass); active alphaC-in conformation for ATP-competitive screening (decisive); resolution (minor tiebreak).

   1M17 and 4HJO both carry the same ATP-site ligand (AQ4, erlotinib, InChIKey AAKJLRGGTJKAMG-UHFFFAOYSA-N) in a single clean chain, and neither has an unobserved residue inside the 5 A pocket, so both pass the hard gate and the pocket definitions are directly comparable. The choice is decided by conformational state. 4HJO's deposition says so in its title ("CRYSTAL STRUCTURE OF THE INACTIVE EGFR TYROSINE KINASE DOMAIN WITH ERLOTINIB"); 1M17's title does not state a state, so both were established from the coordinates via the beta3-Lys721/alphaC-Glu738 salt bridge: 3.05 A in 1M17 (active (alphaC-in, salt bridge intact)) versus 13.3 A in 4HJO (inactive (alphaC-out, salt bridge broken)); erlotinib binding both states is the subject of BIOCHEM.J. V. 448 417 2012. We screen ATP-competitive chemotypes against the catalytically relevant active site, so the active alphaC-in state is the right target, with resolution (2.6 vs 2.75 A) a minor tiebreak in the same direction. Two caveats to state in the proposal. First, 4HJO's pocket is the better-ordered one: mean B 42.9 vs 51.5 over pocket atoms, ligand B 47.5 vs 72.8, and 0 vs 16 alternate-conformer atoms in the site. Second, 1M17 models more residues (312 vs 278) only because its construct is longer (span 672-995 vs 679-960); it actually has more residues in internal chain gaps (12 vs 4), none of them in the pocket. 4HJO is therefore kept as the inactive-state counter-structure for a cross-docking sanity check rather than discarded.

## How these were produced

- Baseline: Morgan r=2, 2048 bits -> RandomForestClassifier(500 trees, class_weight='balanced'), one model per endpoint, 120 (split, seed, endpoint) fits total.
- The seed (0-4) controls both the split and the forest; splits are replayed from the committed index files in `splits/`.
- Scaffold split: 2156 unique Bemis-Murcko scaffolds, largest group 1260 molecules (`c1ccccc1`), 1658 singletons; whole groups never split.
