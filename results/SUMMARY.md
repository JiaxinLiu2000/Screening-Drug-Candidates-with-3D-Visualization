## How these were produced

- Baseline: Morgan r=2, 2048 bits -> RandomForestClassifier(500 trees, class_weight='balanced'), one model per endpoint, 120 (split, seed, endpoint) fits total.
- The seed (0-4) controls both the split and the forest; splits are replayed from the committed index files in `splits/`.
- Scaffold split: 2156 unique Bemis-Murcko scaffolds, largest group 1260 molecules (`c1ccccc1`), 1658 singletons; whole groups never split.
