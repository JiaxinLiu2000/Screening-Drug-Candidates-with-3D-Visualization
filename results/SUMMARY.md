**4. PDB ID to use: 1M17** (over 4HJO)

   Decision criteria, in order: no unobserved residue inside the 5 A pocket (hard gate); active alphaC-in conformation for ATP-competitive screening; resolution; modelled-chain completeness.

   1M17 and 4HJO both carry the same ATP-site ligand (AQ4, erlotinib) in a single clean chain, and neither has an unobserved residue inside the 5 A pocket, so the pocket definitions are directly comparable. They differ in conformational state, which the depositions state explicitly and the coordinates confirm: 1M17 is "EPIDERMAL GROWTH FACTOR RECEPTOR TYROSINE KINASE DOMAIN WITH 4-ANILINOQUINAZOLINE INHIBITOR ERLOTINIB" with Lys721-Glu738 at 3.05 A (active (alphaC-in, salt bridge intact)), while 4HJO is "CRYSTAL STRUCTURE OF THE INACTIVE EGFR TYROSINE KINASE DOMAIN WITH ERLOTINIB" with the same pair at 13.3 A (inactive (alphaC-out, salt bridge broken)); see BIOCHEM.J. V. 448 417 2012. We screen ATP-competitive chemotypes against the catalytically relevant active site, so the active alphaC-in state is the right target, and 1M17 is additionally the higher-resolution (2.6 vs 2.75 A) and more complete model (312 vs 278 modelled residues, 12 vs 4 residues in internal chain gaps). Caveat to state in the proposal: the pocket of 4HJO is the better ordered one (mean B 42.9 vs 51.5 for pocket atoms, ligand B 47.5 vs 72.8, 0 vs 16 alternate-conformer atoms), and erlotinib is known to bind both states, so 4HJO is kept as the inactive-state counter-structure for a cross-docking sanity check rather than discarded.

## How these were produced

- Baseline: Morgan r=2, 2048 bits -> RandomForestClassifier(500 trees, class_weight='balanced'), one model per endpoint, 120 (split, seed, endpoint) fits total.
- The seed (0-4) controls both the split and the forest; splits are replayed from the committed index files in `splits/`.
- Scaffold split: 2156 unique Bemis-Murcko scaffolds, largest group 1260 molecules (`c1ccccc1`), 1658 singletons; whole groups never split.
