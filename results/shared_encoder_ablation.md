## Verdict

**shared encoding does not help, and the chemical-space measurement explains why.**

| comparison | toxicity macro ROC-AUC | affinity Pearson r (random split) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| single-task, from scratch (A / B) | 0.7689 | 0.7310 |
| shared, from scratch (C) | 0.6820 | 0.6907 |
| single-task, pretrained (E / F) | 0.7603 | 0.6791 |
| shared, pretrained (D) | 0.7619 | 0.6737 |
| **effect of sharing, from scratch (C − A / C − B)** | **-0.0869** | **-0.0404** |
| **effect of sharing, pretrained (D − E / D − F)** | **+0.0016** | **-0.0054** |
| shared, from scratch, best loss weight (C @ w=0.1 / w=0.5) | 0.7453 | 0.7563 |
| **effect of sharing at the best weight (C − A / C − B)** | **-0.0236** | **+0.0253** |

Largest seed-to-seed standard deviation used as the noise floor: 0.0310 (toxicity macro ROC-AUC), 0.0658 (affinity Pearson r). A difference is only called an effect if it exceeds that.

**The default loss weight is doing most of the damage.** At weight 1 the shared GIN is clearly worse than single-task (-0.0869 toxicity, -0.0404 affinity), but its best sweep cells reach 0.7453 toxicity (w=0.1, 2 seeds) and 0.7563 affinity Pearson (w=0.5, 2 seeds), both within the seed noise of the single-task configurations. The accurate statement is therefore: sharing at the default weight hurts; with the weight tuned down it is neutral; at no weight does it beat single-task training by more than seed noise. Sweep cells use fewer seeds than the main comparison, so they are the weaker evidence of the two.

Chemical-space context (Task 1): the median nearest-neighbour Tanimoto from Tox21 to KIBA is 0.2597 against within-set medians of 0.6000 (Tox21) and 0.7143 (KIBA) — **weak overlap**. The two task datasets are adjacent rather than overlapping, which is the condition this ablation was restructured to test.

Configurations E and F exist because D alone changes two things at once (pretrained encoder *and* task sharing); without a pretrained single-task control, 'pretraining helps' cannot be told apart from 'sharing helps when pretrained'.
