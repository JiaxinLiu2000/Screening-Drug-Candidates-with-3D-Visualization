## Random -> scaffold ROC-AUC drop

- hERG: -0.0088
- Tox21: 0.0713

**Diverges.** The hERG random-to-scaffold drop (-0.0088) does not reproduce Tox21's 0.0713 (ratio -0.12); hERG therefore does not corroborate the leakage finding. Measured reasons: (i) hERG's test sets hold only 58-59 molecules, and the seed-to-seed std of hERG random ROC-AUC (0.0639) is 7.2x the absolute random-scaffold gap, so the gap is within seed noise; (ii) hERG is far more scaffold-diverse (0.6582 unique scaffolds per molecule vs 0.3510 for Tox21), so a random split already separates test from train chemistry: the random-vs-scaffold gap in mean test-to-train nearest-neighbour Tanimoto is 0.0897 for hERG vs 0.1450 for Tox21. The hERG positive rate is 0.6867.
