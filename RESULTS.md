## 3. Standardization and deduplication

Per SMILES: parse → largest covalently connected fragment (salt strip) → `Chem.RemoveStereochemistry` → canonical SMILES → InChIKey. Deduplication on InChIKey with a per-label majority vote; ties become NaN. Filter: 10–60 heavy atoms and elements {Br, C, Cl, F, H, I, N, O, P, S}.

| step | count |
| --- | --- |
| merged input rows | 7831 |
| dropped, failed to parse | 8 (parse_error=8) |
| rows entering deduplication | 7823 |
| unique InChIKeys | 7580 |
| **duplicate rows merged** | **243** |
| label cells set to NaN by a tied vote | 67 |
| **removed by heavy-atom / element filter** | **1437** (too_few_heavy_atoms=1263, disallowed_element=102, too_many_heavy_atoms=72) |
| **final molecules** | **6143** |

**v1 simplification:** stereochemistry is removed before the InChIKey is computed, so enantiomers and cis/trans pairs collapse to one record. The model therefore cannot express stereo-dependent toxicity; restoring stereo is the first planned change after the proposal.

### Which receptor to use — evidence

Decision criteria, in order: no unobserved residue inside the 5 Å pocket (hard gate, both pass); active αC-in conformation for ATP-competitive screening (decisive); resolution (minor tiebreak).

| PDB | deposited TITLE | primary citation | Lys721–Glu738 | state | unobserved residues in pocket | altloc atoms in pocket | mean B pocket / ligand | internal chain gaps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1M17 | EPIDERMAL GROWTH FACTOR RECEPTOR TYROSINE KINASE DOMAIN WITH 4-ANILINOQUINAZOLINE INHIBITOR ERLOTINIB | STRUCTURE OF THE EPIDERMAL GROWTH FACTOR RECEPTOR KINASE DOMAIN ALONE AND IN COMPLEX WITH A 4-ANILINOQUINAZOLINE INHIBITOR. [J.BIOL.CHEM. V. 277 46265 2002] | 3.05 Å | active (αC-in, salt bridge intact) | none | 16 | 51.5 / 72.8 | 12 [965, 966, 967, 968, 969, 970, 971, 972, 973, 974, 975, 976] |
| 4HJO | CRYSTAL STRUCTURE OF THE INACTIVE EGFR TYROSINE KINASE DOMAIN WITH ERLOTINIB | ERLOTINIB BINDS BOTH INACTIVE AND ACTIVE CONFORMATIONS OF THE EGFR TYROSINE KINASE DOMAIN. [BIOCHEM.J. V. 448 417 2012] | 13.3 Å | inactive (αC-out, salt bridge broken) | none | 0 | 42.9 / 47.5 | 4 [710, 711, 712, 713] |

**Recommendation: 1M17.** 1M17 and 4HJO both carry the same ATP-site ligand (AQ4, erlotinib, InChIKey AAKJLRGGTJKAMG-UHFFFAOYSA-N) in a single clean chain, and neither has an unobserved residue inside the 5 Å pocket, so both pass the hard gate and the pocket definitions are directly comparable. The choice is decided by conformational state. 4HJO's deposition says so in its title ("CRYSTAL STRUCTURE OF THE INACTIVE EGFR TYROSINE KINASE DOMAIN WITH ERLOTINIB"); 1M17's title does not state a state, so both were established from the coordinates via the β3-Lys721/αC-Glu738 salt bridge: 3.05 Å in 1M17 (active (αC-in, salt bridge intact)) versus 13.3 Å in 4HJO (inactive (αC-out, salt bridge broken)); erlotinib binding both states is the subject of BIOCHEM.J. V. 448 417 2012. We screen ATP-competitive chemotypes against the catalytically relevant active site, so the active αC-in state is the right target, with resolution (2.6 vs 2.75 Å) a minor tiebreak in the same direction. Two caveats to state in the proposal. First, 4HJO's pocket is the better-ordered one: mean B 42.9 vs 51.5 over pocket atoms, ligand B 47.5 vs 72.8, and 0 vs 16 alternate-conformer atoms in the site. Second, 1M17 models more residues (312 vs 278) only because its construct is longer (span 672-995 vs 679-960); it actually has more residues in internal chain gaps (12 vs 4), none of them in the pocket. 4HJO is therefore kept as the inactive-state counter-structure for a cross-docking sanity check rather than discarded.

## 8. Known limitations to state in the proposal

1. Stereochemistry is dropped (v1 simplification, Section 3).
2. Labels are sparse: each endpoint covers 4385–5658 of the 6143 molecules; models are per-endpoint with no multi-task sharing yet.
3. Classes are imbalanced (3.1%–18.3% positives); `class_weight="balanced"` is the only mitigation and PR-AUC is the more honest metric.
4. Binding affinity is not modelled yet — Task 5 only prepares the receptor, pocket box and viewer that the docking/affinity stage will consume.
5. The scaffold split uses one global greedy pass; it is not balanced per endpoint, so individual endpoints can have very different positive rates between train and test.
